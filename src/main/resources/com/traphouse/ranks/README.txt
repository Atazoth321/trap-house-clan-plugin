Rank emblems in this directory were cropped from the three OSRS rank screenshots supplied by the TRAP HOUSE owner in this task. Original pixel shapes and colors are retained on transparent PNG backgrounds.

Progression (low to high): Recruit, Pawn, Corporal, Novice, Sergeant, Colonel, Brigadier, Admiral, Marshal.
Management/staff (Owner-assigned only): Owner, Deputy Owner, Master, Major, Proselyte.

Displayed staff ranks are separate from backend permissions and do not grant service authority.
